# Growth Audit — ArtEasy

**Live:** https://dkobzar-12345.github.io/arteasy-flow-rebuild/
**On a phone:** https://dkobzar-12345.github.io/arteasy-flow-rebuild/?open=proto

Five ranked ideas for ArtEasy's iOS onboarding and paywall, plus a clickable prototype built in ArtEasy's own design. Prepared by Adapty, November 2026.

| # | Idea | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Ask "learn or fun" before the price, and let the paywall headline follow the answer | CR +15–20% |
| 02 | Show their first sketch before the paywall, matched to that answer | CR +10–15% |
| 03 | Ask for data consent, tracking and notifications after the paywall, on the home screen | CR +10–15% |
| 04 | Swap the rating request on screen four for your App Store rating on the paywall | CR +10–15% |
| 05 | Show the offer straight from the paywall's X, leading with 9,99 € vs 14,99 € a week | ARPU +10–15% |

Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps — not measured lift for ArtEasy.

**Grounding.** Every price, rating, quote and feature line comes from ArtEasy's own App Store listing and in-app screens. Screens are translated into Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese by us; prices stay as shown on ArtEasy's screens and reviews stay verbatim.

**Run locally:** `python3 -m http.server 8000` in this folder, then open http://localhost:8000.
